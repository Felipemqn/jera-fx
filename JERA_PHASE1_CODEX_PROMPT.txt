- Investment Mode

- organized database
- reproducible models

- No hardcoded market values in frontend or backend logic.
- Prefer official sources, especially Banco Central do Brasil for PTAX, SGS and Focus / Expectations.

- Every model run must be versioned and reproducible.
- The platform must be suitable both for direct client presentation and for internal investment work.
